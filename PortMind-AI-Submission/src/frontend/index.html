The Problem

Port congestion costs the industry billions every year

Large ports handle hundreds of vessels and thousands of containers daily. When arrivals exceed available capacity, congestion cascades across the entire supply chain.

⏱
Vessel Waiting Time
Vessels queue for hours — sometimes days — when berths are congested. Each waiting hour costs thousands in demurrage and port fees.
📦
Yard Overflow
Container yards fill up. When yard utilisation exceeds 85%, operations slow dramatically, delaying unloading and truck collections.
🏗
Crane Under-utilisation
Cranes sit idle at empty berths while congested berths lack capacity. Poor allocation multiplies delays across the terminal.
🌩
Disruption Cascades
A single crane failure or weather event can cascade across 20+ vessels. Port operators often react too late, without decision support.
📊
Fragmented Data
Port operations rely on spreadsheets and disconnected systems. Operators lack a single view of risk, capacity, and recommended action.
🔗
Supply Chain Disruption
Port delays ripple into roads, warehouses, factories and retail. The downstream cost of congestion exceeds the port cost by orders of magnitude.

Key Features

Everything an operations team needs

Six integrated modules, one unified platform. Each module provides AI-assisted recommendations, not just raw data.

Operations Dashboard
Real-time port status, KPI overview, congestion risk score, live port map, and AI recommendations — all on one screen.
Vessel Risk Intelligence
AI-scored risk for every vessel (0–100), with transparent factor-by-factor explanations and berth assignment recommendations.
Berth Optimisation
Visual berth timeline, congestion detection, and AI-powered re-planning that shows current vs. optimised schedules side by side.
Crane Operations
Live crane utilisation monitoring, overload alerts, maintenance tracking, and intelligent crane-to-vessel assignment suggestions.
Yard Intelligence
Zone-by-zone capacity monitoring, critical threshold alerts, and AI recommendations for container redistribution before overflow.
AI Crisis Simulator
Simulate disruptions (weather, strike, crane failure) and instantly see the projected impact — and the AI response plan — before they happen.

How It Works

Five steps from data to decision

PortMind AI is designed for operations managers who need answers immediately — not after digging through reports.

1
Ingest Port Data
Vessel schedules, berth assignments, crane status, yard utilisation, and disruption alerts feed into the platform.
2
AI Risk Scoring
Each vessel and berth receives a transparent risk score based on multiple operational factors — updated continuously.
3
Congestion Forecast
The system projects congestion over the next 24, 48, and 72 hours, highlighting emerging bottlenecks before they form.
4
Optimisation Engine
AI generates optimised berth, crane, and container plans — with side-by-side comparisons against the current plan.
5
Operator Decides
Every recommendation includes a reason, expected impact, and confidence level. The operator stays in control.
